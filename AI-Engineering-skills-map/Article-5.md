## Part 5: Shaping the Build

## Author: Andrew.Nd
## Book: The AI Engineering Skills Map


### Summary 


Shaping the build focuses on an engineer's ability to influence what gets developed, why it is needed, and how it delivers value. AI engineers should not only implement technical instructions but also understand user problems and contribute to product decisions. One important skill is driving the build loop, which involves  gathering feedback, testing solutions, and making continuous improvements. This skill requires a high degree of agency, in which you identify opportunities, prioritize what matters, and act on them. Additionally, you know how to take accountability for issues that arise, act in the face of ambiguity, persist through setbacks, and measure your work not just by task completion, but according to the value you create.  Communication and leadership are important because successful development requires collaboration with designers, product managers, stakeholders, and other professionals. Another essential quality is high-agency ownership, which involves identifying problems, proposing solutions, taking initiative, and accepting responsibility for outcomes. Ultimately, engineers should measure success by the value their products deliver to users rather than  the number of tasks completed.
