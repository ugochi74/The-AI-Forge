## Part 2: Building and Deploying AI Applications

## Author: Andrew.Nd
## Book: The AI Engineering Skills Map

### Summary

Building and deploying AI applications requires understanding how AI models work and how to integrate them into reliable software systems. AI engineers should understand large language models (LLMs). They must also understand how to connect models to external information using techniques such as Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), embeddings, and vector search. Another important area is agentic systems, which allow AI models to perform tasks using tools, workflows, and memory. 
Engineers must evaluate AI outputs through testing, human feedback, and error analysis because model responses are not always predictable. They must also understand production requirements such as monitoring, security, latency, and cost optimization. Machine learning foundations, including supervised learning, reinforcement learning, and neural networks, provide additional technical knowledge. The main objective is to develop AI applications that are accurate, useful, secure, and reliable in real-world environments.
