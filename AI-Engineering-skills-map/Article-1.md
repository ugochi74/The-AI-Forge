## Part 1: Overview of the AI Engineering Skills Map

## Author: Andrew.Nd
## Book: The AI Engineering Skills Map


### Summary 

AI engineering combines artificial intelligence knowledge, software development, and problem-solving skills to build useful applications.
The AI Engineering Skills Map identifies four major pillars: building and deploying AI applications, software engineering fundamentals, using coding agents, and shaping the build. These pillars represent the essential competencies required to become an effective AI engineer. 
Building AI applications involves understanding models and integrating them into real-world systems. Software engineering provides the foundation for developing reliable, secure, and maintainable applications. 
Coding agents help automate development tasks but require proper direction and supervision. Shaping the build involves understanding user needs, making product decisions, and taking responsibility for outcomes. Together, these skills show that AI engineering is not simply about writing prompts or using AI tools. It requires technical knowledge, practical experience, continuous learning, and the ability to deliver solutions that solve real problems.
